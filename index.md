## Welcome to my Notes

You're most likely future me. I hope you'll find these collection of categorised notes
useful. You've probably come here because you did some work on a different
tech stack (even for a few days!) and have completely forgotten Phoenix and Liveview.
Not to worry, you've come to the right place. Just navigate to the right category and
hopefully you'll find what you need.

If you're not me, you're welcome to browse too! I
used to make gists for the things I fiddle with but the docs just got too long and too difficult to navigate. I'm hoping with these MystMD docs that 
it'll be easier to search them and also navigate using categories.

This won't be about "teaching" but more about refreshing memory. I think this is
different than a reference because I'm assuming a future me that has some vague
notions of things and also, I'm anticipating a bit of what I'd be trying to do
and my usual setup.
